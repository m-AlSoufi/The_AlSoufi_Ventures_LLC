About Our Practice

I am Mohammad Al-Soufi, founder of The Al-Soufi Ventures, LLC. With over 5 years of dedicated experience in U.S. immigration law support, I am committed to providing precise, reliable, and compassionate paralegal services. My practice operates under the direct supervision of a licensed attorney, ensuring all work meets the highest professional standards.

I specialize in guiding families, individuals, and businesses through complex processes like VAWA, SIJ, employment-based petitions, and adjustment of status—turning daunting paperwork into a clear, manageable path forward.
